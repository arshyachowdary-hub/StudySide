Why StudySide?

School does not happen separately from the rest of a student's life.

StudySide is designed around the idea that academic planning should be flexible when a student's energy, stress, or circumstances change.

Academic planner

Track assignments, tests, projects, priorities, deadlines, and estimated completion times.

Daily check-in

Record how you feel and what may be affecting your ability to focus.

Bad Day Mode

Make the workload more manageable by prioritizing smaller tasks.

Support

Find trusted adults and read moderated student experiences.
