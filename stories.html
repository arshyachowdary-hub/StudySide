Student Stories

You are not the only student who has a difficult week.

This prototype shows how moderated student experiences could give current students perspective and encouragement.

Example story

“I stopped trying to fix everything at once.”

I had several deadlines at the same time and was also dealing with things outside school. Making a short list of the most important tasks helped me stop feeling like I had to finish everything immediately. Talking to a teacher also made a difference.

Example story

“Asking for help did not mean I had failed.”

I thought I had to handle school stress by myself. I eventually told a trusted adult that I was struggling. They helped me work out what could realistically be done first.

Example story

“Small steps made a big assignment less scary.”

Instead of putting “finish project” on my list, I wrote down the first few things I needed to do. Once the first step was done, the next step felt more possible.

Example story

“I learned that my workload could change.”

Some days I could do a lot and other days I could not. Planning a smaller version of my workload on difficult days helped me focus on what was most important.

How stories would work in a real app

  • Students choose what information to share.
  • Stories are moderated before appearing publicly.
  • Personal identifying details are removed or discouraged.
  • Students can report unsafe or inappropriate content.

Important boundary

Stories provide perspective and connection. They are not professional medical advice, a diagnosis, or a substitute for talking to a trusted adult or qualified professional.
