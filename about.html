About StudySide

Support the student, not just the schedule.

StudySide is a student support web app for students whose academic work is affected by stress, problems at home, personal difficulties, extracurricular pressure, or mental-health challenges.

The problem

Normal planners assume students have the same amount of time and energy every day. Personal circumstances can make a normal workload feel much harder.

The solution

StudySide combines flexible academic planning, wellbeing check-ins, Bad Day Mode, AI-assisted organization, trusted support, student stories, and weekly reflection.

Who it is for

Students who want a practical way to organize schoolwork while recognizing that wellbeing and personal circumstances can affect their capacity.

Core features

Academic planner

Add assignments, tests, projects, priorities, due dates, estimated time, and smaller subtasks.

Daily wellbeing check-in

Select a feeling and factors affecting the day, such as workload, sleep, school, home, or other difficulties.

Bad Day Mode

A lighter planning approach that surfaces short, high-priority tasks.

AI StudySide Assistant

Helps break work into steps, create plans, and draft messages. It is not a therapist and does not diagnose.

I Need Someone

Identify people the student already trusts, such as a parent/guardian, teacher, counselor, or relative.

Weekly reflection

Review what caused stress, what helped, and one thing to try differently next week.

Safety and privacy direction

A real production version would need secure accounts, privacy controls, story moderation, reporting tools, consent processes, and professional review of wellbeing and AI features.

StudySide — student support prototype.

Not a replacement for professional mental-health care.
